Alex Joneston has been abducted by Arasaka mercs at the direction of Petrochem. They took him via AV to the NC docks, where he and the mercenaries boarded a Piranha's cargo submarine. The submarine carries the mercs and Petrochem's private cargo (Luxury goods and foods, precursor chems, data chips with corporate espionage and tech blueprints), as well weapons, drugs, smash, cheaper food, plant seeds, and construction materials.

From there, they submerged and went out into the pacific for most of a day, docking at one of many large oil rigs. This constellation of rigs all ostensibly belong to Petrochem, but the numerous floating docks and shanty towns affixed to them tell a different story. 

Of the seven rigs, only a single one has a hardline to the military complex below, which is the real seat of Petrochem's corporate intelligence services. The demimonde above is tolerated as it gives cover to the secretive operations below, as well as providing a good interface to the global economy. The elevator is masked as a rather thick drill casing stretching down seven thousand fathoms to the sea floor, four times deep than most submarines can operate. Specialized diving bells hang under the other rigs, providing emergency escape for any corporate operatives caught out in the demimonde.
